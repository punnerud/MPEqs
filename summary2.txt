PHASE 50 — the guard injection as a reusable module, and where it must not live
================================================================================

The wrap from phase 49 is now `experiments/loopmem/rretl_guard.py`: wrap_int_guard for the
text transform, define_guarded_function for one function, create_guarded_residual_lens for
the whole recipe — guard the forward and rex, define the inverse bare, register, return the
engine's probe count. Six tests pin it, including the motivating case end to end against a
real database: the offset pair refused unguarded (the Float(1.3e17) precision trap) and
registered guarded. The guardwrap experiment rewired through the module reproduces 3/4
exactly, and the module test joined `make all`, skipping honestly when the mpedb build is
absent rather than passing vacuously.

It was first written INTO mpedb — a guard.rs in mpedb-spell, a facade method, unit tests —
and then pulled back out on the owner's instruction to keep mpedb's core untouched, with all
custom machinery living in LLMdb. That boundary is worth the paragraph, because it is the
same boundary every phase of this thread has drawn. mpedb is the verifier, and a verifier
that carries convenience helpers for the things it judges is a verifier whose independence
has started to erode. The guard is OUR discipline for OUR authors; the engine's job is to
refuse what is wrong regardless of who wrote it or what wrapped it. The wrap changes who
writes the guard. It must never change who checks it.

The subset itself needed no extension for this — the guard is expressible in what PySpell
already has, and the reversibility core (residuals stored per row, verified round trips)
is untouched throughout.

================================================================================
PHASE 51 — plan choice as retrieval: the store built free, and where lookup stops
================================================================================

The reframing under test: a plan is not something to think up but something to look up.
Read the problem, strip its numbers, map the residue against embeddings of generalised
solved types, bind the values, execute exactly. Thinking as plan search versus the store as
plan search.

THE STORE COSTS NOTHING TO BUILD AND VERIFIES ITSELF. GSM8K training solutions carry
calculator annotations — <<48/2=24>> — from which the record extracts plans mechanically,
chains steps by value flow, executes each plan and keeps it only if it reproduces the
training answer, then generalises under the phase 45 round-trip rule. From 2,000 training
solutions: 1,554 verified generalised plans, collapsing to 599 distinct plan shapes, largest
type 64 members. Compression into groups, with zero model calls.

PURE LOOKUP FAILS, AND THE AUTOPSY IS WORTH MORE THAN A WIN. Sixty test problems, mask,
embed, retrieve nearest compatible template, bind positionally, execute:

    compatible template found     56/60
    solved by top-1 lookup         0/60
    solved by vote over top-25     1/60

Two separate defects, either fatal alone. RETRIEVAL FETCHES TOPIC, NOT TYPE: baking matched
baking and fruit matched fruit at cosine 0.6-0.7, while the plan shapes had nothing in
common — a bakery inventory problem bound into a flour-budget plan returns
-5.6e15/2.8e14. And BINDING IS SEMANTIC, NOT POSITIONAL: even a right-type neighbour lists
its numbers in a different order, so v1-to-first-number scrambles the roles. The margin
profile confirms both: 33 of 55 votes tied at margin 0, and the single case with margin >= 5
was also the single correct answer — agreement stays a truthful confidence signal even
while the answers themselves are wrong.

THE MISSING INGREDIENT IS THE MODEL'S ONE IRREPLACEABLE JOB. Phase 44 measured exactly this
mapping — numbers into a shown plan, by role — at 19/20 for the 35B model. So the division
stands, sharpened: the store generalises and verifies, the embedding aims, the solver
computes, and the model does the two things nothing else can — write a new plan, and bind
roles into a retrieved one. (The retrieval-aimed ADAPT arm runs when the GPU frees.)

AND THE 20-MINUTE AIME ANSWER, from the same sitting: with thinking on at 1,600 tokens,
4 of 6 thoughts never closed — think-solo 0/6 measures the budget, not the reasoning — and
the three graph plans that ran computed the wrong quantity entirely (42, 91, 34,460 against
12, 751, 738). On olympiad problems the plan is the hard part, there is no inline check
against a text to refuse a wrong plan with, and an executor cannot rescue what a planner
got wrong. Graph+solver helps where the errors live in arithmetic; on AIME they live in
the reasoning, which is the boundary of the whole method stated by measurement.

================================================================================
PHASE 52 — units as a routed graph: sixteen facts, 7,652 conversions, all exact
================================================================================

Units are nodes, each stored conversion an exact rational edge, and converting is ROUTING:
find the path, multiply the factors. A compound unit is two routes at once — "3 miles per
second in km/h" is the mile-to-km path over the second-to-hour path, second -> minute ->
hour, giving 10863072/625 = 17,380.9152 km/h exactly, as a fraction, no model anywhere.

    stored edges                          16, over 20 units in 4 dimensions
    simple conversions routed             94 ordered pairs (5.9x the edges)
    speed units                           40 distinct, 1,560 conversions among them
    all two-dimension rates            7,652 conversions (478x the edges)
    reverse route exactly reciprocal   94/94, verified pair by pair
    rate spot-checks against hand      4/4 exact

THE COMPOSITION CLAIM, MEASURED. Every edge composes with every other, so coverage is
quadratic per dimension and multiplicative across compounds: 16 written-down facts route
7,652 rate conversions, each exact and each reversible by construction — the reverse route
IS the reciprocal, which is the residue rule holding with an empty residual. This is the
generalisation-compounds-exponentially argument in its cleanest measurable form.

DIMENSIONS AS THE CONTROL. Quantities carry dimension vectors; addition demands identical
ones, multiplication adds them. Rate x time = distance accepted, distance/time = rate
accepted, miles + hours refused BEFORE any arithmetic, 2/2 and 2/2. It cannot say a plan is
right; it refuses a class of wrong ones for free — the same asymmetry as every verifier in
this thread, and precisely the check phase 51's word-problem plans lacked.

The model's only role left in this subsystem is reading which units a sentence mentions.
Everything after that is the graph's.

================================================================================
PHASE 53 — the machinery on real problems: where it wins, and where solo does
================================================================================

The full no-thinking comparison, 20 GSM8K and 15 AIME problems:

                          solo    graph+solver    majority-of-3
    olmoe-1b  gsm8k      16/20        4/20            15/20
    qwen-35b  gsm8k      19/20       16/20            19/20
    qwen-35b  aime        0/15        0/15             0/15

ON REAL WORD PROBLEMS, SOLO BEATS THE GRAPH ARM — the reversal of phase 45, and the honest
boundary of the whole method. On synthetic expressions the plan was trivial and the
arithmetic was the failure mode, so removing arithmetic from the model tripled the small
model's score. On word problems the PLAN is the failure mode: the graph arm must commit to
a full plan blind, never seeing an intermediate value, while solo's brief working lets the
model see 3 * 12 = 36 before deciding what to do with it. Planning needs the feedback that
executing denies it, and there is no inline check against a text to refuse a wrong plan.
The record's division of labour helps exactly where errors live in arithmetic — measured
three times now (phases 45, 51, here) from three directions.

THE AGREEMENT SIGNAL IS THE PIECE THAT SURVIVES EVERYTHING. Three differently-phrased
attempts, across both models and both datasets:

    all three agree      18 cases, 18 right    (16/16 qwen gsm8k, 2/2 olmoe gsm8k)
    they disagree        37 cases, 16 right

Unanimity was right 18 of 18 times on real mathematics, and on AIME it NEVER fired — the
signal correctly withholds confidence everywhere the answers are garbage. Combined with
phase 34 (9/9 against 1/9), agreement-across-entries is now the most robust finding in the
project: a grader-free acceptance gate at 100% observed precision, whose claim rate falls
to zero exactly where claiming would be wrong.

That is also the escalation policy stated as measurement: accept when the attempts agree,
spend more only when they do not — help only on failure, from the cheapest rung up. And a
caveat for the tally: the 1B model's 16/20 solo is higher than its published range, on a
20-problem sample with a generous last-number extraction; the ordering of the arms is the
finding, not the absolute percentages.

================================================================================
PHASE 54 — the dimension control wired into lookup: it refuses, and refusal was
not the bottleneck
================================================================================

Phase 52's control, between retrieval and execution: units read mechanically from the text
beside each number, every candidate plan bound with dimensioned quantities, refused if any
step adds distinct known units or the result unit is not the asked one.

    tagger noise floor      1,112/1,554 store templates pass on their own source (72%)
    candidates refused      440 of 1,300 as ill-typed (34%)
    top-1 unfiltered        0/60
    top-1 type-clean        1/60
    vote among type-clean   1/60

THE CHECK WORKS AS A REFUSER. A third of all retrieved candidates are provably ill-typed
for the problem's numbers — adding loaves to dollars — and are removed before any
arithmetic, for free, no model involved. The mixed-addition rule and the asked-unit rule
both fire exactly as built.

THE TAGGER'S NOISE FLOOR IS 28%, AND IT IS INFORMATIVE. GSM8K units are ad-hoc nouns, and
the noun beside a number is not always what the number counts: "sold clips to 48 of her
friends" tags 48 as friends when it counts clips. A dimension control is only as good as
its unit reader, and on informal text the unit reader is a language problem — which is to
say, the model's problem, one more item on its short list alongside plan writing and role
binding.

AND ACCURACY BARELY MOVES, 0/60 to 1/60, BECAUSE FILTERING IS NOT BINDING. Remove every
ill-typed candidate and the survivors are still positionally bound, and a same-unit
scramble types perfectly. The control eliminates a class of garbage; it cannot order what
remains or map roles. The three-way division from phase 51 stands with sharper edges:
the store generalises, the embedding aims, the control REFUSES — and the two jobs left,
binding roles and reading units off informal text, are both the model's.

Where the control belongs next is not retrieval but the graph arm of phase 53: a
model-WRITTEN plan whose steps mix units is wrong before execution, and that is a
refusal-with-a-reason the plan writer can be shown — the same shape as mpedb's
counter-examples.

================================================================================
PHASE 55 — the refusal gate on model plans: only as good as its ground truth
================================================================================

Phase 54 ended by pointing the dimension control at the graph arm: refuse a model-written
plan whose steps mix units, name the step and the units, let the model retry — mpedb's
counter-example shape, on word problems. Built and measured, with every refused plan ALSO
executed post hoc so each refusal is classified true (the plan was in fact wrong) or false
(the control shot down a right plan):

    olmoe-1b   graph 4/20 -> graph+dim 1/20    27 refusals: 20 true, 7 false, 1 fixed
    qwen-35b   graph 16/20 -> graph+dim 16/20  36 refusals:  4 true, 32 false, 11 fixed

THE GATE DOES NOT PAY HERE, AND THE REASON IS THE GROUND TRUTH, NOT THE SHAPE. For the
strong model 32 of 36 refusals were FALSE — the tagger's 28% noise floor firing on plans
that were right — and eleven retries only clawed back what the gate had wrongly taken. For
the weak model the refusals were mostly true (20 of 27) but it could not use them: one fix
in twenty-seven reasons shown, and the false refusals cost it three of its four correct
answers. Net: the small model got worse, the large one broke even.

Against phase 48-49 the contrast is exact. mpedb's verifier refused with PERFECT ground
truth — every counter-example real — and there the loop was safe for everyone and the only
question was who writes the guard. Here the verifier itself is 72% reliable, and a noisy
refusal gate is worse than none for a model that cannot recover and pure friction for one
that did not need it. The counter-example shape transfers; its value does not survive a
noisy judge. A refusal gate inherits the reliability of whatever it trusts, and unit
reading on informal text is a language problem — the model's problem — sitting underneath
a control that was supposed to check the model.

================================================================================
PHASE 56 — combining formulas as routing, and the consumption rule, again
================================================================================

The proposal: formulas compose, so input-to-solution is ROUTING in dimension space. A
problem's quantities carry units, the question names a target unit, and the plan is a path
built by combining quantities under the type rules — searched streaming, the frontier
deduplicated by dimension signature, never materialising the expression tree.

FIRST RUN: 40 of 40 dimension-rich problems "ambiguous". The extra candidates were all
self-combinations — a derived value recombined with itself, x - x = 0, x + x = 2x — which
is EXACTLY the rule the workpad enforced in phase 13: each given is consumed once. Source
masks on every state, disjoint-only combination, and:

    dimension-rich problems (speed, price, density, rate x time, with distractors):
        unique route 40/40, correct 40/40, distractors self-excluded, zero model calls

    GSM8K:
        untaggable 26, no route 13, ambiguous 12 (truth present 1), unique 9 (right 2)

IN ITS DOMAIN THE LEVER IS TOTAL. Speed = distance/time is not retrieved or asked for — it
is DERIVED from the units, the only dimensionally-legal combination of what the problem
provides, and the distractor quantities exclude themselves by failing to route. That is
"kombinere formler for å komme frem til svar" as a theorem-shaped mechanism: given clean
units, composition plus uniqueness IS the plan.

OUTSIDE ITS DOMAIN IT IS NEARLY MUTE, for the reason predicted before the run: count-heavy
problems carry three numbers of the same unit, dimensions cannot distinguish them, and the
28% tagger noise poisons what little grip remains. The same triage that solves 40/40
synthetic rate problems solves 2/60 GSM8K.

The consumption rule surfacing a third time (workpad phase 13, template round trips phase
45, dimension routing here) marks it as one of the project's few universals: every
fabricated candidate in every domain came from using the same thing twice.

================================================================================
PHASE 57 — the model reads the units, and the abstraction question splits in two
================================================================================

Phase 55 ended on: unit reading is a language problem sitting under the control. So the
language model reads — one call per problem abstracts the story into quantities-with-units
plus the asked unit — and every downstream mechanism reruns unchanged. Every delta is the
reader's.

    GATE (20 problems, 35B plans):
        heuristic judge   16/20 -> 16/20,  36 refusals, 32 FALSE
        model-read judge  16/20 -> 17/20,  23 refusals, 18 false, 5 true, 7 fixed, 0 unreadable

    ROUTING (60 problems):
        heuristic   26 untaggable, unique 9 right 2
        model-read   0 untaggable, unique 9 right 3, ambiguous 40 (truth present 5)

    PLANS FROM THE ABSTRACT FORM ALONE:  2/20, against 16/20 from the story

THE READER FIXES WHAT READING CAN FIX. "Sold clips to 48 of her friends" now tags 48 as
clips, the implicit 2 in "half" arrives as a unitless factor the regex never even saw, and
zero problems are unreadable. False refusals nearly halve (32 to 18) and the gate turns net
positive for the first time (+1 with 7 fixed-after-refusal). What remains false is mostly
COMPOUND units — a wage is dollars per hour, not dollars, and a one-noun-per-number reading
cannot say so. The next rung is reading rates as rates, and it is a reading problem again,
not a control problem.

THE ABSTRACTION HALF SPLITS CLEANLY. As input to the JUDGE, abstraction is pure gain — that
is the whole phase. As input to the PLANNER it is catastrophic: 2/20 from quantities-and-
units alone against 16/20 from the story. The story is not detail-noise around a problem;
it IS the problem — who did what to what carries the plan, and stripping it leaves numbers
with no relations. In residue terms: the narrative is the residual of this compression, and
it was load-bearing. Abstract for checking, never for planning.

Routing after perfect reading still solves almost nothing on GSM8K (3/60), which closes the
question cleanly: reading was the blocker for TAGGING, and count-heavy structure is the
blocker for ROUTING, and no reader fixes the second — three numbers of clips give
dimensions nothing to grip whoever reads them.

================================================================================
CORRECTION to phases 20 and 26 — "Y = 3 reverses perfectly and contributes null"
================================================================================

The judgement was made under one objective and stated as if under all. Under phase 20's
own measure — the widest expression the model must hold — substituting Y for a bare
number changed nothing (9.0 -> 8.3 tokens, 2/20 against 3/20), and that half stands:
reversibility alone cannot tell a compressing substitution from a null one, for THAT
objective.

But "contributes nothing" was wrong as a general claim, and the project's own later
measurements are the correction. Putting a value into a variable is the generalisation
move itself: applied to every literal it is exactly the template form (v1 + v2 * v3) / v4
— which took the 1B model from 5/60 to 14/60 on hits (phase 45, the first memory that
measurably helped), collapsed 1,554 solved instances into 599 reusable shapes (phase 51),
made repair-by-provenance possible at all (phase 46, wrong bindings detectable and
correctable because names exist to rebind), and made the store rot-proof, since a graph
of variables carries no wrong arithmetic to remember.

The reconciliation, one line: a substitution is judged against an objective, and Y = 3 is
null for compressing an instance and foundational for generalising across instances. Same
operation, two ledgers. The phase 26 one-liner "reversibility is safety, not progress"
survives only with "toward the stated objective" appended — variable introduction was
progress all along, on the ledger the thread ended up caring most about.

================================================================================
PHASE 58 — absorb, don't strip: the full translation costs nothing
================================================================================

Phase 57 measured abstraction-as-deletion at 2/20 and the correction was the proposal
itself: absorb EVERYTHING — every quantity a named node, every relation a definition, the
question a distinguished node — until the residual of the translation is empty. One model
call per problem; the record solves the system topologically with Fraction arithmetic and
verifies it structurally, with perfect ground truth: every problem number present, the
asked node defined, no cycles, everything reducing to arithmetic.

    qwen-35b   relation graph 16/20   — exactly the story-plan baseline (16/20)
    olmoe-1b   relation graph  3/20   — its baseline is 4/20, within run noise
    (quantities-only, the deletion:    2/20)

THE ABSORPTION IS FREE. The full translation loses nothing against planning from the
story, and unlike the story it yields an object the record can check exactly and solve
exactly. Structural refusals with named reasons ran through the same retry shape as
everything else, and this judge — unlike the unit tagger — cannot fire falsely.

================================================================================
PHASE 59 — the staged version: reversibility total, solving costlier, repair real
================================================================================

The same absorption in ORDER, each step reversible with its residue kept: the model
quotes one span and the definition it becomes; the record cuts the span (exact match or
refusal), stores (span, position, its numbers) as the step's residue, adds the definition
to the graph. Three runs, each fixing one mechanical defect, the trajectory itself the
record:

    run 1   0/20   the ASKED call: ten echoes of the example name "total", eight
                   unparseable — the example-echo trap declared in the plan and then
                   repeated in that one prompt
    run 2   7/20   the record reads the asked node off the graph's own shape instead:
                   the SINK, the one node no other definition references — deterministic,
                   no model call
    run 3   9/20   sink fallback when the model's fallback name is not in the graph, and
                   missing-number refusals repaired by one absorption step aimed at the
                   RESIDUAL text, where an unabsorbed number by definition still sits

Final: 9/20 solved, 10 structural refusals, 4 localised via provenance, 2 repaired in
place without touching the rest of the graph.

WHAT IS TOTAL: the reversibility. 109 of 109 steps restored the previous text byte for
byte when their span was put back; all 20 full chains unwound LIFO to the original
exactly; 97% of the problems' numbers were absorbed; and 86 span misses were refused by
the record without one corrupt cut reaching the text. The rRETL contract held on text
with zero exceptions — the residues ARE the story, kept, and the graph and the residues
can each reproduce the other.

WHAT IT COSTS: solving. 9/20 staged against 16/20 one-shot, and the gap is coherence —
each staged step sees only the remaining text and the graph so far, and relations that
span sentences are exactly what a span-at-a-time reader fragments. One call sees the
whole story; five calls each see a fifth of it.

So the two forms divide by purpose, like everything else in this thread divided. One-shot
absorption for solving. Staged absorption for when the trail matters more than the score:
every step undoable, every node owning the sentence it came from, every structural
refusal traceable — and two of the four traced refusals actually repaired in place, the
first in-place text-provenance repairs in the project. The order was the price of the
audit trail, and both numbers are now known rather than argued.

PHASE 60 — the ADAPT arm: role binding works, and the examples aim it wrong
================================================================================

The missing mapstore arm: retrieval fetches the two nearest solved problems, shown whole
as worked examples, and the model binds the new problem's numbers into the roles. Three
arms per model on the same 60 problems, within one run:

                 solo    graph cold    ADAPT (retrieved examples)
    qwen-35b    51/60       45/60            41/60
    olmoe-1b    37/60       15/60             4/60

RETRIEVED EXAMPLES HURT, BOTH MODELS, AND THE SMALL ONE CATASTROPHICALLY. The phase 51
diagnosis holds with the model in the loop: retrieval finds TOPIC, not type, and a model
shown a wrong-type plan follows it — binding roles into the wrong plan is worse than no
plan. Phase 44's 19/20 adaptation was to same-shape templates; these neighbours are
often the wrong shape, and the 1B model rides them down (15 to 4).

What survives: ADAPT sits far above every record-only path through the same store (0/60,
1/60, 1/60), so the binding CAPABILITY is real — it was the aiming that poisoned it. The
store has now failed to earn its keep on word problems in five separate arms, which is
the honest verdict on text-similarity retrieval of plans: until retrieval is by TYPE,
showing what it fetches makes things worse, not better.

Also banked: solo at 60-problem scale confirms phase 53's reversal in both models
(51 vs 45, 37 vs 15) — on word problems the model's own working beats the blind plan.

PHASE 61 — the model diagnoses itself: grounded, and ranked by salience
================================================================================

The goal stated: the model improves through new tasks, so someone must decide what the
next task is. The record's gap list was written down BEFORE asking (role binding,
compound units, staged coherence, thinking budget, the named fill-failing template), the
model got six raw result files, and wrote its top five.

    keyword first pass:  5/5 items matched gaps — FLATTERING, the matcher credited
                         filenames ("modelunits") as substance
    hand audit:          5/5 items cite REAL numbers, zero fabrication
                         1/5 hits a record gap in substance (the truncated thoughts)
                         the biggest number in its own data — mapstore solved 0/60 —
                         was overlooked in favour of 106 extraction failures from the
                         SAME FILE (106 of 2,000, near-zero leverage)

The failure mode being tested for — inventing plausible gaps the data does not show —
DID NOT OCCUR. What occurred instead is subtler and decides the loop's design: the model
ranks by how conspicuous a counter is, not by how much fixing it would gain. 86 span
misses (refused safely, cost calls not correctness) outranked a 0/60. Salience is not
leverage.

So the improvement loop closes with the division this whole thread kept finding: the
model DRAFTS the diagnosis — grounded, honest, useful as a reading of the data — and the
record's counters RANK it, because ranking requires knowing what a number costs, and
cost lives in the ledger, not in the prose. The next task comes from the top of the
record's list with the model's reading attached, not the other way round.

================================================================================
PHASE 62 — the cascade: the right gate with no deliverable to gate
================================================================================

The proposal: at 1B scale the model never solves — it picks a formula shape and fills the
values, the record executes, agreement between two 1B paths accepts, disagreement
escalates to 35B. On the 60 problems:

    tier 1 accepted             0/60   (formula arm produced an answer 4/60)
    shape menu coverage         104/1,554 store plans (7%)
    escalated                   60/60, right 51 — identical to 35B everywhere
    time                        825s against 474s for 35B alone: 0.57x, SLOWER

Two structural causes, both previously measured. GSM8K's diversity means eight commonest
shapes cover 7% of the store (599 shapes over 1,554 plans — phase 51's number recast), so
perfect picking could not have fit most problems. And the 1B production-yield bottleneck
(phase 47's 18/30) put the formula arm at 4/60 answers; without a second deliverable there
is no agreement, without agreement no acceptance, and tier 1 becomes pure cost. The gate's
FORM is right — phase 53 measured unanimity at 100% precision — but a gate needs two
arriving deliverables, and on word problems the 1B has one (solo, 37/60).

================================================================================
PHASE 63 — paraphrase fan-out: errors multiply across the legs
================================================================================

One problem in, two or three self-contained subquestions out, a combining formula, the 1B
answering every leg, the record combining exactly:

    1B decomposes,  1B answers    12/60      (1B solo: 37/60)
    35B decomposes, 1B answers    28/60      (35B solo: 51/60)

EVEN THE STRONG DECOMPOSER LANDS THE CHAIN BELOW THE SMALL MODEL ALONE. Three legs plus a
combine is four chances to fail where solo has one; at roughly 85% per leg and 75% split
quality the product is about 0.46, which is where 28/60 sits. Model-authored subquestions
are paraphrases, and phase 57 already measured what paraphrase costs — "self-contained" is
an instruction, not a property. The one-expensive-decomposer-many-cheap-solvers shape does
not pay when the legs are prose.

================================================================================
PHASE 64 — rRETL fan-out: the split is perfect and the residual is in the names
================================================================================

The reversible form: the RECORD cuts at sentence boundaries — 20/20 splits rejoin
byte-exact, mean fan-out 3.4, residual empty by construction — and each part translates to
definitions given the graph so far. Calls drop from ~10 per problem (staged, with 86 span
misses) to exactly the sentence count. And:

    qwen-35b   2/20      olmoe-1b   0/20      (one-shot: 16/20, model-cut staged: 9/20)

The autopsy names the killer precisely, and it is the proposed HIDDEN RESIDUAL in a form
the byte check cannot see: 7 of 20 failures are "a definition does not reduce to
arithmetic" — part 2 references `april` where part 1 named the node `april_clips`. The
split is lossless on the text and lossy on the NAMESPACE. Four more failures are numbers
whose part translated to nothing on the single pass, and the 8 structurally-"ok" runs
mostly picked the wrong sink once question-sentence noise entered the graph.

================================================================================
PHASE 65 — fan-out, compress, fan out again: iteration does not pay this residual
================================================================================

The cycle as proposed: every part translates given the compressed graph, rounds repeat
until a fixpoint, forward references resolvable in round 2 because compression made the
context global. Measured:

    solved after round 1        0/20
    solved at fixpoint          1/20
    late-arriving definitions   6 of 77  — the hidden residual, counted
    mean rounds 2.2, 148 calls  — against ONE call and 16/20 for one-shot

Iteration pays almost nothing here, and the reason closes the loop on the diagnosis: the
residual is not missing VALUES (only 6 definitions arrived late) but mismatched NAMES, and
re-asking does not rename. A record-side compression step that merges aliases between
rounds — mechanical, round-trip-checked — is the untested remedy, and it is the record's
job by every rule this thread has established.

WHAT THE WHOLE FAN-OUT FAMILY SAYS, one line per member: join arity was never the lever
(19); paraphrase legs multiply errors (63); a perfect reversible split still loses the
namespace (64); and iteration cannot pay a residual that is referential rather than
material (65). Fan-out PAID exactly twice in this project — digit columns (17) and
document navigation (29-42) — and both times the residual between parts was tiny and
mechanically carriable: a carry digit, a byte offset. That is the law the family obeys:
fan out when the coupling between parts fits in the record's hand, and see the whole
problem at once when it does not.

================================================================================

The project's own instrument turned inward: moetrace reads the router's expert choices per

Two consequences, both actionable at the record level — where every lever in this project
has ended up:

neighbour subtractions (a borrow turns 4-3 into repeated near-neighbour work), so the digit
decomposition was steering the model into precisely its weakest cells. The floor was never
"single-digit arithmetic" — it was a two-cell-wide strip of the subtraction table, and now
it has coordinates.

PHASE 76 — assume, ledger, solve: the pending questions become a solver's input

ASSUME AND LEDGER. Open questions must not block calculation: at a two-sided edge the
LOWER branch is taken (conservative, declared), the assumption is ledgered with its
address and its counterfactual carried to FINAL form, and the result runs flagged
PENDING. An answer reruns exactly the ledger's dependents — 3 of 5 computations touched,
2 skipped, counterfactuals reproduced to the digit, the toll question still pending as it
should be. Laziness keeps the streaming (alternatives exist only at edges actually hit);
provenance does the scheduling.

MANY AT ONCE IS A BINARY SOLVER. k distinct open questions make the pending space 2^k
assignments — and one observed outcome SOLVES it: a path crossing the quote twice and the
toll once has two distinct questions (an edge crossed twice is ONE question, learned the
usual way — the first enumeration gave each hit its own slot and a both-flipped combo
partially cancelled into a phantom survivor), four assignments, and exactly one reproduces
the observation: the ground-truth directions, all questions answered at once.

COUPLED UNKNOWNS ARE AN ACTUAL SOLVER. Speed against weight: p = m*v and E = m*v*v/2 with
m, v unknown is no assignment enumeration — it is equations. The dimension machinery

v = 150 m/s, m = 20 kg, both equations verified. Tier entered only when questions share
variables.

PHASE 80 — first steps in algebra: the residue prices the road, exactly

    divide early (normalise the lead at once)        1,061

Multiplying both sides first — the proposal verbatim — is 4.9x cheaper in carried residue

PHASE 83 — solved roads become memory; pushback refines the vocabulary

The session's stated loop — learn and store, then search what was done before — closed

refinement machinery never fired — the autopsied zero: self-parameterising ops made
every class one class.

ACT TWO attacked it: a class where gathering x first is strictly cheaper than clearing
(lead difference exactly 1, halves everywhere) and whose four base bits are IDENTICAL to
the clear-first class. Two audit lessons, both measured:

  THE STRIDED AUDIT SAMPLED ONE CLASS ONLY. Every-4th-serve auditing on an alternating
  stream landed always on the fairly-priced half: twenty overpayments served, zero
  caught. A stride sharing a factor with the world's pattern is blind; the audit was
  made RANDOM (rate 1/4) and caught the overpayment after two serves.

  THE VOCABULARY RAN OUT, SO THE MEMORY MINTED. The split machinery exhausted all four
  base bits (identical signatures) and coined a new one from the predicate library —
  lead_one, the first predicate separating the two exemplar states — appended it to its

  keys, depths 4-5-5, deepened ONLY where the world pushed back. 40/40 exact throughout;
  hard failures 0; the one miss refilled a hole mechanically.

Learning measured twice over: the search bill collapsing toward the audit floor, and
the memory coining the word for a distinction it could not previously say. Exactness
never moved: 120/120 across both acts and transfer.

PHASE 88 — translation memory: the agreement toll is paid once per template

================================================================================
PHASE 90 — answer plus record-executable check: the mechanical gate holds its zero
================================================================================

The replacement phase 89's law demanded: instead of two attempts agreeing (correlated

attributes) that the record executes. And because the model authors the check, the
record audits the auditor with DECOYS: deliver only if the check accepts the answer
AND rejects all six decoys (answer±1, ±7, two seeded randoms). Vacuity dies
mechanically; circularity is counted by suspicion marker and priced in the open.

  DEMANDING THE VERIFIER LIFTED THE SOLVING. Answers right outright: 6/10 — against

  Formalising the conditions disciplines the solve. (Confound declared: one prompt,
  so verifier-demand and prompt-variation cannot be fully separated; the gap is 3x.)

  THE MECHANICAL GATE HELD ITS ZERO. Delivered 1, right 1, WRONG 0, flagged 9 —
  against the agreement gate's delivered 3, right 1, WRONG 2. No vacuous check

The ladder's retry rung (the record names its refusal, the model rewrites only the

PHASE 92 — MPEqs' generic solver library: the record grows by CLASSES
================================================================================

Phase 91 gave the record a formula library — one relation per entry. This gives it a
SOLVER library: parameterised machines for whole problem classes, each taking a typed
spec (never code), executing exactly in Fractions or integers, refusing by name, and
reporting its work so a runaway search dies instead of hanging.

    15 solvers: search, linear_system, quadratic, crt, gcd_lcm, series,
    combinatorics, recurrence, rate_work, ratio_split, mixture, interest,
    base_convert, digit_ops, factor
    22 search predicates, 45 wordings (the lexical face, for embedding proposal)
    22/22 self-test cases exact, 6/6 refusals named and correct

The load-bearing one is SEARCH, because so many olympiad questions are "the <aggregate>
of all n in <domain> such that <conditions>": a domain (integer range, divisors of N,
divisors of k! via Legendre so 13! is never built), a conjunction of predicates, an
aggregate, and a post-op — which is exactly how AIME's "answer mod 1000" is said.

AND IT SOLVES A REAL AIME PROBLEM BY CHAINING, WITH NO MODEL AT ALL. "The sum of all m
with 13!/m a perfect square is 2^a 3^b 5^c 7^d 11^e 13^f; find a+b+c+d+e+f": search
returns 9,698,458,770, factor returns 2·3²·5·7³·11·13⁴, the exponent sum is 12 — the
published answer, and the problem phase 89's solo arms got right only 2 times in 10.

with k=13 still demanded an "n", and the dispatcher reported the KeyError honestly as
an error rather than an answer). The tally across the study is now 7 hand-anchor errors
against 1 machinery error, and the machinery error was caught by the anchors — which is
the whole argument for writing truths before code.

================================================================================
PHASE 95 (built before phase 94's evidence, deliberately) — the library grows by
expressions: tuples, polynomials, coordinates, modular arithmetic
================================================================================

Phase 92's search has a fixed predicate list and ONE variable, which is exactly where
competition problems escape it: they ask about pairs and triples related by arithmetic
nobody enumerated in advance. The general move is to let conditions be EXPRESSIONS,
written as ordinary strings — because format is capability (phase 84), and
"a*a + b*b == c*c" is something any model can write while nested predicate JSON is not.

Safety is phase 90's discipline reused: the string goes through Python's own parser and
is walked against a whitelist of node types and function names, so what executes is
arithmetic over bound variables and nothing else.

    multisearch  1-4 variables with ranges, optional strict ordering, expression
                 conditions tested AS SOON AS their variables bind (so triples are
                 feasible), an objective expression, aggregates, mod post-op
    polynomial   exact rational roots (verified, not guessed), Vieta, evaluation
    geometry     exact coordinates: squared distance, shoelace area, collinearity,
                 circle through three points, line intersection
    modular      modpow, inverse, multiplicative order, totient

    15/15 self-test cases exact, 6/6 refusals named — three of them attempted
    escapes from the expression sandbox (an __import__ call, an attribute access,
    a variable-exponent power), all refused by the AST walk before evaluation
    MPEqs now holds 19 solvers; expressions admit 14 functions over up to 4 variables

Written and self-tested BEFORE the coverage sweep of phase 94 ran, so that phase's
named-missing list is an honest test of whether this growth was aimed at the right
thing — a prediction, registered by timestamp in the repository, not a rationalisation.

Thirty real AIME problems, the whole 15-solver catalogue in the prompt, and one

    claimed a mapping      22/30   (declined 5, still unparseable 3)

Three things follow, none of them comfortable:
